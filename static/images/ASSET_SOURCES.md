# Logo and icon sources

These assets identify the linked resources and the author’s affiliations. Institutional marks retain their official colors, shapes, and aspect ratios; their trademark rights are unaffected by the website’s MIT license.

- `logos/epfl.svg`: official EPFL web asset, https://static.epfl.ch/latest/images/logo.svg; identity guidance: https://www.epfl.ch/about/overview/identity/.
- `logos/idiap.svg`: unmodified `LOGO/DIGITAL (RGB)/SVG/IDIAP_LOGO_EN_BLUE.svg` from the official 2026 logo package, https://idiap.ch/cms/api/media/file/logo-idiap-2026.zip; source: https://idiap.ch/en/press-resources.
- `icons/github.svg`: GitHub mark from Primer Octicons, https://github.com/primer/octicons/blob/main/icons/mark-github-16.svg; license in `icons/OCTICONS-LICENSE.txt`.
- `icons/arxiv.svg`: arXiv mark from Simple Icons, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/arxiv.svg; library license in `icons/SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.txt`. arXiv branding: https://info.arxiv.org/brand/brand-guidelines.html.
- The PDF document and storage-device icons are inline SVG drawn for this website and inherit its MIT license.

The self-hosted TeX Gyre Termes typeface matches the text font embedded in the original study overview PDF. Its four unmodified OpenType faces and upstream GUST Font License, README, and manifest are in `../fonts/`. Package source: https://ctan.org/pkg/tex-gyre.
